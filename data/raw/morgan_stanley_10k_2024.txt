PART I - ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS
================================================================================
1. Market Volatility and Macroeconomic Sensitivity:
Net revenues in Institutional Securities and Investment Management are highly dependent on global market conditions, interest rate environments, equity trading volumes, and corporate M&A advisory activity. Prolonged market downturns could adversely affect advisory fees, underwriting spreads, and asset management performance fees.

2. Cybersecurity, AI Security, and Technology Infrastructure Resilience:
The Firm relies heavily on sophisticated technological platforms to process millions of complex financial transactions daily across global exchanges. Threats of cyber attacks, unauthorized data exposure, and vulnerabilities associated with adopting generative artificial intelligence systems represent critical operational risks.

3. Stringent Capital, Liquidity, and Regulatory Mandates (Basel III / Dodd-Frank):
The Firm is subject to strict regulatory supervision by the Federal Reserve, SEC, FINRA, and international regulatory bodies. Heightened capital requirements, stress testing mandates (CCAR), and compliance audits require substantial operational and compliance infrastructure.
